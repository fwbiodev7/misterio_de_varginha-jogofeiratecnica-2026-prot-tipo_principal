# Escola de referência e iluminação noturna

Recriação da foto de 30/09: sala aberta, 12 mesas brancas (9 com computadores), 12 cadeiras azuis, três janelas com cortinas, lousa à direita, projetor, mesa do professor e nove gaiolas em uma grade de três por três. As posições dos alunos são compartilhadas entre o gerador da cena e o controlador de execução.

O atlas `Assets/Resources/Varginha/SchoolComputerLab.png` tem oito recortes nomeados, configurados por `VarginhaSchoolComputerLabImporter.Configure()`. Os nomes e IDs são cadastrados juntos para impedir o retorno aos recortes automáticos e aos móveis de madeira.

`Assets/Resources/Varginha/OutdoorLanternPost.png` é uma lanterna suspensa em suporte de madeira. A altura é 2,3 unidades, mantendo a mesma escala horizontal e vertical. Há quatro postes na escola e três pontos de iluminação no quintal da casa, incluindo o poste existente. A sombra azulada do exterior deixa os interiores livres e abre áreas de luz quente junto às lanternas.

As duas imagens foram geradas pela ferramenta integrada ImageGen, com transparência, e copiadas para Resources. Prompts finais:

## Móveis

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a single production sprite ATLAS for an existing Unity 2D top-down Brazilian school computer classroom circa 1996. Output a transparent PNG, landscape 2:1 aspect ratio, EXACTLY 4 columns x 2 rows of equal square cells (8 sprites total), each sprite confined to its own cell with transparent margins. All sprites in consistent classic SNES RPG pixel art, orthographic slightly overhead front elevation (not isometric), crisp black/dark brown one-pixel outlines, logical 64x64 pixels per cell enlarged nearest-neighbor, no blur. FIRST ROW left-to-right: (1) wide ivory laminate school TABLE with light rounded rectangular tabletop, dark thin metal frame, four narrow gray legs, completely EMPTY tabletop; (2) exactly the same TABLE with a black 1990s desktop computer on top: dark blue-black CRT monitor on LEFT, tiny dark keyboard in front of it, tall black tower on RIGHT, tiny mouse, NO chair; (3) a small cobalt BLUE school CHAIR facing SOUTH, blue seat and backrest above seat, slim gray metal legs, fully isolated, NO table; (4) wide rectangular WHITEBOARD facing front, thin dark gray frame, clean offwhite surface, tiny blue black red erasers/markers on bottom rail. SECOND ROW left-to-right: (1) wide night WINDOW, three blue glass panes in charcoal thin frames, ivory CURTAINS gathered on both sides, minimal sill, night dark blue hills through glass; (2) narrow tall gray metal OPEN BOOK SHELF front view with three dark shelves and a few colored books, muted gray casing; (3) small ceiling PROJECTOR unit front/overhead view, offwhite rectangular box, dark black lens and subtle gray top mount, isolated; (4) a wide TEACHER TABLE light gray/offwhite top with slender dark gray metal legs, empty. Match a modest 1996 Brazilian computer lab, avoid medieval wooden furniture. Objects occupy about 82% of their cell width (chair 48%) and 68% height, centered exactly in each cell, all foot baselines aligned at 82% cell height except wall-hung objects centered vertically. No background, room, characters, cages, letters, grid borders, shadows outside silhouettes, labels, floor, duplicates or extra objects. Genuine alpha transparency around every sprite.

## Poste

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE isolated upright outdoor lamp-post sprite for a 2D top-down pixel-art Brazilian RPG. Dark walnut wooden vertical support on LEFT, short sturdy horizontal crossarm at TOP reaching RIGHT, a small iron hook and chain suspending an ornate black/aged-brass lantern on RIGHT. Lantern has a compact pointed roof, four bright warm ivory/yellow glass panels, tapered dark frame and a small bottom finial. Closely resemble a cozy classic RPG hanging street lantern: believable stout wood and a modest glass lantern, no stretched pole or elongated glass. Natural silhouette roughly 1:2 width:height, full object about twice the height of an RPG character. Slight overhead front-facing orthographic view, not isometric. Crisp hand-pixelled dark outlines, brown and gold palette, logical64x128 pixels enlarged nearest neighbor style. Entire support and lantern visible, transparent background and ample clean transparent margins. NO ground, road, grass, large cast shadow, glow circle, labels, multiple lamps or frames. Glow inside glass only. Produce a transparent PNG.
